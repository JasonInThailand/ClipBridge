# ClipBridge Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

ClipBridge is a clipboard manager. This policy explains what it does with your data.

## What ClipBridge stores

- Items you copy (text, rich text, images, and file references) are saved in a local database on your PC,
  inside the app's own data folder.
- Text found inside copied images is read with the OCR engine built into Windows, on your PC, so images become searchable.
- Settings, including your sync key, are saved in the same local folder.

History is kept until you delete it, clear it from the tray menu, or it ages out of the history limit.
Use "Pause capturing" in the tray menu when you do not want something recorded.

## What leaves your PC

Nothing is sent to the developer or to any cloud service. ClipBridge has no analytics, telemetry, ads, or accounts.

If you turn on sync, clipboard items are sent directly to your other PC running ClipBridge on the same local network,
encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from the sync key you set. They never pass through any server.

## Your control

Uninstalling ClipBridge removes its data folder. You can also delete individual items or clear all unpinned history at any time.
